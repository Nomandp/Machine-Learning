Project Objectives

Academic underperformance and attrition in higher education are frequently identified too late in a semester to execute effective remedial action. This project addresses that challenge by delivering:

  • Proactive Risk Prediction: Classifying students into At Risk vs. On Track using supervised learning trained on authentic historical student demographic and examination data.
  • Behavioral Persona Clustering: Applying unsupervised K-Means clustering to uncover latent student segments based on study duration, absences, and exam trajectories.
  • Actionable Early Warning Interventions: Automatically generating personalized remedial strategies (tutoring, attendance monitoring, study schedule adjustment) rather than mere static predictions.
  • Production Web Architecture: Delivering a responsive Flask, SQLite, Bootstrap 5, and Chart.js portal supporting full CRUD record management and live model inference.
Core System Highlights & Impact

Engineered to provide an actionable bridge between data-driven machine learning models and academic advising:

Detailed Mapping to DUET ML Practical Syllabus
Lab 01
Data Preprocessing & Scaling
  • Exploratory data analysis on UCI Student dataset.
  • Binary encoding of categorical indicators.
  • StandardScaler normalization of feature variables.
  • Zero Data Leakage: Target grade (G3) strictly isolated from training features.
Lab 03
Logistic Regression
  • Sigmoid activation function mapping linear combinations into probabilities [0, 1].
  • Cross-entropy loss minimization.
  • Evaluation via Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC curve.
  • Serves as calibrated probabilistic risk estimator.
Lab 05
Random Forest Ensemble
  • Bootstrap Aggregation (Bagging) across 100 decision trees.
  • Sub-feature randomness to reduce model variance and prevent overfitting.
  • Feature Importance analysis (G2, G1 & failures identified as primary drivers).
  • Primary production classifier (91.1% accuracy).
Lab 10
K-Means Clustering
  • Unsupervised behavioral grouping on study habits, absences & grades.
  • Elbow Method evaluation confirming optimal k = 3.
  • Persona discovery: High Achievers, Moderate Watchlist, and High Academic Needs.
  • Informs targeted institutional intervention plans.

Ethical Considerations in Educational AI

Deploying predictive artificial intelligence in academic settings requires ethical rigor:

  • Supportive Rather than Punitive: The system's purpose is early support and remedial assistance, never punitive grading or gatekeeping.
  • Model Explainability: Predictions are paired with feature importance and specific behavioral factors (e.g. absences, study time) so faculty understand why an alert was triggered.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Oversight: Faculty advisors and mentors retain full discretion in approving and executing intervention plans.
  • Privacy & Security: Student data is stored in localized SQLite records without external third-party data tracking.
